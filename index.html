Why Pre-Cut Meat Is Ruining Your Curry

When meat is cut hours or days in advance, it bleeds out natural juices, absorbs moisture, and turns rubbery. Here is how KATTA's live butcher process ensures juicy flavor every single time.

01

You Pick Your Cut

Choose exact piece sizes (Curry Cut, Biryani Special, Boneless, Keema) and trimming preferences on the app.

02

Ticket To Noida Hub

Your ticket prints at the nearest Noida cutting station. Whole chilled carcass is brought to the sanitized wood block.

03

Hand-Cleaved & Cleansed

Master butchers portion with razor-sharp surgical blades, rinse with pure RO water, and remove excess fat.

04

Chilled Gate Delivery

Vacuum packed at 0-4°C with dry ice packs and dispatched to your Noida sector in 45-60 minutes flat.
